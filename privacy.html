Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Dhanvika ("we", "us"), 102 Air Bypass Road, Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, runs this website. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: when you create an account or sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive your name and email address. We do not receive your Google or Facebook password.
  • Booking details: when you reserve a table we store your name, phone number, date, time, number of guests and any special requests. If you are signed in, we remember your phone number to fill in your next booking.
  • WhatsApp: bookings open WhatsApp so you can send them to us. Messages you send there are handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

How we use it

Only to manage your table bookings, contact you about them, and make booking quicker next time. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored securely with our service providers Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting). Only the restaurant owner can see customer and booking details.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account and all your information at any time, including data received from Google or Facebook. Email digitalpavan61@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp (+91 94909 96614) from the email or phone linked to your account. We will delete it within 7 days and confirm when it's done.

You can also remove Dhanvika's access from your Google account (Security → Third-party connections) or Facebook (Settings → Apps and Websites).
