Our Story

Cooking with fire, honestly

Dhanvika began as a single clay tandoor and a simple idea: great food needs few ingredients, handled with care. Since 2015 we have built relationships with local farms, fishermen and foragers, and let the seasons write our menu.

Everything is cooked the traditional way, from tandoor-grilled starters to slow-simmered biryanis, giving each dish a depth of flavour you can taste.

Chef Daniel Marsh

Trained in kitchens across India and abroad, Daniel returned to open Dhanvika. His cooking is rooted in tradition and driven by what's best this week.

  • 100% traditional tandoor cooking
  • 25+ local suppliers
  • Seasonal menu, changed monthly
